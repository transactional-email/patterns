# Failed payment

## When to send

Card decline or failed renewal. Be clear, calm, and actionable—include update-payment link.

## Sample HTML

```html
<p>We couldn’t process your payment for {{PLAN}}.</p>
<p><a href="{{BILLING_URL}}">Update payment method</a></p>
```
